In the kitchen

Made by hand, start to finish.

From the first ball of dough to the last sealed box, it all happens in our kitchen in Malé.

  1. 01

    Shaped one at a time

    Every bun is rolled, swirled or knotted by hand. No machines, no shortcuts.

  2. 02

    Glazed and criss-crossed

    A glossy glaze, a swirl, a chocolate lattice. Then a little rest before the oven.

  3. 03

    Baked golden

    Out of the oven the morning you get them. That's the whole secret.

  4. 04

    Boxed and sealed

    Each bun gets its own clear box and our label, so it arrives looking the way it left.

Malé & beyond

Made for island days.

Ordering from an island? We'll bring your buns to your boat while it's in Malé. Just pick your boat from the list when you order. Delivery in Malé is a small fee; Hulhulé and Hulhumalé are a little more.

How it works

Ordering takes a minute.

  1. Pick a day

    Pre-order by the evening before. Missed it? On the day, grab the extras while they last.

  2. Delivery or pickup

    Delivery in Malé is a small fee, a little more to Hulhulé and Hulhumalé, or straight to your boat. Pickup is always free.

  3. Pay and send the slip

    Bank transfer, or scan the QR with the BML app. Then upload a screenshot of your payment.

  4. That's it

    Your order is confirmed as soon as the bank shows your payment. We'll take it from there.
